# DIGEST content/Приключение/
## SCOPE
17-18 Markdown files, ~1,294 lines. Two kinds of files: published narrative chapters and draft GM prep notes.

- `Подготовка к партии 2.1/2.2/3/4/5/6.md` (140-154 each), `Post Mortem.md` (173, largest file).
## EXPORTS
- Chapter: no frontmatter, prose body, ends with the navigation line `[[Prev|<- Предыдущая глава]]   [[Next|Следующая глава ->]]`; `___` as separator; `%% ... %%` for GM notes.
- Prep note: frontmatter `draft: "true"`, fixed section set `## Таймлайн`, `## Текущие сюжетные линии`, `## Новая информация для прогресса`, `## Неигровые Персонажи`, `## Запланированные столкновения`, `## Начало Партии`.
- Post-mortem: `draft: "true"`, one `### [[Chapter]]` block per session with `#### Время / Плюсы / Минусы / Выводы`.
## CONVENTIONS
- Filenames are `Глава N[.M]. Title.md`, `Подготовка к партии N.md`, `Подготовка общая.md`, `Post Mortem.md`.
- A double dot at the end of a filename (`...безопасности..md`, `...Симиан..md`) is deliberate: it lets a wikilink end with a period and still resolve.
- There is no `index.md` in the folder - folder listings are generated by the `folder-page` plugin.
- Published chapters carry no frontmatter; every prep/post-mortem file carries `draft: "true"` and is therefore excluded from the build.
- Chapters link out to `Фракции/`, `Локации/`, `Лор/`, `Предметы/`, `Главные герои/`.
## ANTI-PATTERNS
- The "Выводы" sections of `Post Mortem.md` are the vault's standing DM rules, not diary notes: approach rooms as problems rather than fixed combat encounters, improvise from the dice rather than from planned outcomes, state the goal of a scene up front, tell players what is expected of them, let players act inside the narrative. Recorded failure modes to avoid: "Почти не пользовался правилами", "Бой затянулся", "Не использовались союзники", "Бои были однобокими".
- `Бихолдер Зантар` (see `content/Персонажи/`) must never be dropped onto the players directly - the character is designed to wipe a first-level party.
- Do not publish a prep note by accident: keep `draft: "true"` until the content is campaign-ready.
## HOTSPOTS
`Post Mortem.md` (173) is the source of the vault's DM rules. The "Фил / День 1 / День 2" log is copy-pasted verbatim into `Подготовка к партии 4/5/6.md` and `content/Главные герои/Фил.md` - editing one copy does not update the others.
## COMMANDS
