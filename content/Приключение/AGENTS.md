# AGENTS.md - content/Приключение/

17 tracked Markdown files, ~1,294 lines. Two file kinds: published narrative chapters and draft GM prep notes. Vault-wide rules live in `../AGENTS.md`.

## FILE KINDS

**Chapters** - `Глава N[.M]. Title.md` plus the prequel `Пиратка Эстер в Стране Высоких Гор, Зелёных Лесов и Милых Симиан..md`.
- No frontmatter, so the chapter is published.
- Body is prose; `___` separates sections; `%% ... %%` carries GM notes.
- Each chapter ends with the navigation line `[[Prev|<- Предыдущая глава]]   [[Next|Следующая глава ->]]`.
- A double dot at the end of a filename (`...безопасности..md`, `...Симиан..md`) is deliberate: it lets a wikilink end in a period and still resolve.

**Prep notes** - `Подготовка общая.md`, `Подготовка к партии 2.1/2.2/3/4/5/6.md`.
- Frontmatter `draft: "true"`, so the file is excluded from the build.
- Fixed section set: `## Таймлайн`, `## Текущие сюжетные линии`, `## Новая информация для прогресса`, `## Неигровые Персонажи`, `## Запланированные столкновения`, `## Начало Партии`.

**Post-mortem** - `Post Mortem.md` (173 lines, the largest file in the folder).
- `draft: "true"`; one `### [[Chapter]]` block per session with `#### Время / Плюсы / Минусы / Выводы`.

## CONVENTIONS

- `Подготовка общая.md` is the shared prep hub; `Подготовка к партии 7.md` links it as `## [[Подготовка общая]]`.
- There is no `index.md`; the folder listing is generated by the `folder-page` plugin.
- Chapters link out to `Фракции/`, `Локации/`, `Лор/`, `Предметы/`, `Главные герои/`.

## ANTI-PATTERNS

- The `#### Выводы` sections of `Post Mortem.md` are standing DM rules, not diary entries. They are binding: approach rooms as problems rather than as fixed combat encounters, improvise from the dice instead of executing a planned outcome, state the goal of a scene up front, tell the players what is expected of them, let the players act inside the narrative instead of railroading them. The recorded failure modes to avoid are "Почти не пользовался правилами", "Бой затянулся", "Не использовались союзники" and "Бои были однобокими".
- The "Фил / День 1 / День 2" log is copy-pasted verbatim into `Подготовка к партии 4.md`, `5.md`, `6.md` and into `content/Главные герои/Фил.md`. Editing one copy does not update the others.
- Keep `draft: "true"` on prep and post-mortem files. Removing it publishes GM material to the site.
- `Бихолдер Зантар` (see `../Персонажи/`) must never be confronted directly by the players - the design assumes he leaves the scene.

## HOTSPOTS

`Post Mortem.md` (173) is the source of the vault's DM rules. `Подготовка к партии 6.md` (154), `5.md` (145) and `4.md` (140) hold the most recent session state. `Глава 2.2. Сфера Канона.md` (99) is the longest chapter and closes with `%% Повышение уровня до 2 %%`.

## COMMANDS

No local tooling. `npx quartz build` publishes the chapters and hides the drafts; `npx quartz build --serve` previews them.
