Alt+A - add adversary
Alt+E - add encounter

Ctrl+\[ - add link to the selection, or start a link
Ctrl+\| - generate fantasy random staff

Ctrl+P - open command palette

for inline **Fantasy Content Generator** to work:
- Disable and re-enable **Fantasy Content Generator**.
- **Switch to another note/tab and then back to your note.**
- Type `@`.
- Choose something with the arrow keys and Enter.

Ctrl+Shift+R - translate

For spell check to work, vpn is needed.
